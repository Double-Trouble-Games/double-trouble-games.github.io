<b><color=#FFDD44>Thank you for participating in the Playtest!</color></b>
The primary purpose of the current test is to ensure the game is stable on a variety of systems, and no hard gating bugs or data corruption issues exist.

Full game goes to 100, but hero levels in the Demo are hard capped at 35, and the final area you may access is Sinister Shoals.
